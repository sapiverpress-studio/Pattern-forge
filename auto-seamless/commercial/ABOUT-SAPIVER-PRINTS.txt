ABOUT SAPIVER PRINTS

Sapiver Prints is an independent UK creative project from Sapiver Press.

We create practical, design-led artwork and digital resources for people making useful products of their own — from fabrics and accessories to stationery and print-on-demand merchandise.

We work one design at a time. Our goal is to supply well-described files and clear commercial permissions rather than large quantities of poorly checked designs.

Our workflow combines digital tools, individual review and experimentation. Some artwork is produced with the aid of AI; we aim to describe this accurately. Embroidery-style images are not represented as physically hand-stitched or machine stitch-ready files unless they actually are.

We distinguish the contents of each listing and recommend repeat and production checks before manufacturing.

Thank you for supporting an independent creative project.
