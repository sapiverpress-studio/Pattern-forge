# Knitted Fabric texture sources

All images are stored inside `knit/textures/`, so the deployed tool has no third-party image fetch at runtime.

- **Grey chunky knit:** Meshy [Grey chunky knit](https://www.meshy.ai/textures/fabric/grey-chunky-knit), 2K JPG, CC0. Base for stockinette, raised V-knit columns and Fair Isle yarn loops.
- **Cream cable knit:** Meshy [Cream cable knit](https://www.meshy.ai/textures/fabric/cream-cable-knit), 2K JPG, CC0. Actual crossed cable structure.
- **Rust-orange knitted wool:** Meshy [Rust orange knit](https://www.meshy.ai/textures/fabric/rust-orange-knit), 2K JPG, CC0 (ambientCG sourced). Used as the recessed, purl-like wool fibre/relief source for rib and alternating seed-stitch bumps; colours normalized to the chosen main yarn.

Rib and seed are **photo-composites** that combine knit V loops with purl-like raised wool detail in their respective stitch-chart arrangements. These are visual fabric simulations, not verified hand-knitted 2x1 rib or seed stitch swatches. Fair Isle is a photo-based V-loop colourization following a symmetric repeat chart—not printed flat colour polygons.

**Licensing:** All three retained images are described as CC0 by their source pages (checked 6 Nov 2026). The redundant procedural rib/seed imported images were rejected on visual quality and are not distributed in this version.

Images are intended for printed visual designs, not PES/DST or garment knitting instructions. Source licensing does not confer exclusivity in finished recoloured patterns.
